Agentic Systems

Reliable agent workflows, not prompt-only demos.

I bring production discipline from computer vision and edge deployment into applied LLM and multimodal systems. The focus is workflow decomposition, explicit state, constrained outputs, failure handling, evaluation, and maintainable handoff.

Workflow

Perception, context retrieval, candidate generation, selection, validation, and human review.

Reliability

JSON Schema, provider routing, retries, fallbacks, reproducible traces, and cost-quality checks.

Evaluation

Hard cases, A/B comparison, explicit acceptance criteria, regression sets, and review reports.
