Forward-Deployed AI

This track is about turning AI capability into a real workflow: discovery, scoping, prototype design, deployment, evaluation, and handoff.

  • Translating user workflows into technical requirements.
  • Deciding when a prototype is production-ready enough to deploy.
  • Codifying repeatable deployment patterns after customer engagement.

Vision Model Deployment

The real problems in model deployment often live in formats, operators, I/O, post-processing, and hardware differences.

  • Debugging paths from PyTorch to TFLite / ONNX.
  • Validation checklists before shipping on-device vision models.
  • Latency, memory, and accuracy trade-offs in mobile inference.

Model Compression

Quantization is not a switch. It is a set of trade-offs across model architecture, calibration, operators, hardware, and acceptable accuracy loss.

  • When PTQ is enough, and when QAT becomes necessary.
  • Debugging order for INT8 accuracy regressions.
  • Balancing model size, latency, and accuracy.

Camera AI Lab

The value of camera AI ultimately appears in the image. This track uses public examples to discuss bokeh, depth, segmentation boundaries, composition, and enhancement.

  • Why AI bokeh is hard at subject boundaries.
  • Engineering constraints of monocular depth in camera effects.
  • State-machine design for real-time composition guidance.

Agentic Vision

The point of agentic vision is not to make a model talk more. It is to make visual decisions constrainable, comparable, and reproducible.

  • Designing evals for VLM-based composition agents.
  • Comparing quality and cost in multi-model A/B tests.
  • Engineering boundaries for retry logic and rule-based fallbacks.
