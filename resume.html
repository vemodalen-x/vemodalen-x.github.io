Junxian Wu

Senior AI Engineer focused on edge vision systems, model compression, computational photography, and agentic multimodal workflows.

Profile

I focus on moving vision models into real device and product constraints: training, compression, export, on-device inference, rendering quality, evaluation, and maintainable deployment. My experience covers segmentation/matting, monocular depth, AI bokeh, real-time composition, portrait enhancement, and multimodal composition agents.

Selected contributions

  • Built reusable training-to-deployment workflows for edge vision models, connecting model development, quantization, export, and deployment validation.
  • Led on-device real-time composition work that combines detection, tracking, and state-machine guidance for live camera interaction.
  • Worked on depth-aware computational photography, translating segmentation and depth signals into controllable bokeh and portrait rendering effects.
  • Designed multimodal agent workflows for visual composition, with structured output, retry logic, and evaluation hooks for model comparison.
  • Integrated deep models with GPU rendering and service pipelines using OpenGL/GLSL, TensorRT, Docker, and FastAPI.
