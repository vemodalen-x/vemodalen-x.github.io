About

I build forward-deployed AI systems for vision and multimodal workflows.

Engineering taste

  • Start from the workflow. A useful AI system starts with the operational problem, not the model demo.
  • Deployment is part of modeling. Architecture, I/O, quantization strategy, and runtime constraints should be designed together.
  • Quality lives in edge cases. Camera quality is won in difficult lighting, motion, occlusion, boundaries, and device variation.
  • Agents need eval, not vibes. Multimodal decisions should be constrained, compared, reproduced, and debugged.
