## Deliberate Credibility Choices

- The headline begins with the actual title "Senior AI Engineer" and uses "Forward-Deployed AI" as a capability direction.
- Unsupported terms such as "industry-leading" were removed.
- Unpublished customer, device, accuracy, latency, cost, and shipment figures were excluded.
- Customer names, private project names, repository names, model artifacts, thresholds, paths, and logs remain excluded.
- Responsibilities were rewritten as delivery outcomes without inventing numerical impact.

## Remaining Gaps

### Quantified impact

The public profile has limited verified business or performance metrics. This is preferable to fabricated numbers, but one or two approved metrics would strengthen screening. Only add metrics that are documented and cleared for public use, such as a latency range, model-size reduction, release cadence, evaluation coverage, or engineering time saved.

### Generic full-stack FDE roles

The profile is strongest for AI deployment, computer vision, edge ML, and multimodal workflows. It does not currently demonstrate deep JavaScript/frontend or enterprise data-platform delivery. Do not claim those skills merely to match generic Forward Deployed Software Engineer roles; prioritize AI-focused FDE and technical deployment positions.

### Customer-facing scope

The evidence supports collaboration with product, QA, pre-sales, and customer-facing stakeholders. Avoid claiming long-term on-site customer embedding unless you can describe a real example in an interview.

### Years of experience

The verified profile supports 4+ years of professional AI engineering experience. Some senior FDE roles request 5+ years; treat those as selective stretch applications rather than changing the profile to an unsupported duration.
